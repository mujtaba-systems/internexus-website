Meet Our Global Faculty & Mentors

At Internexus Global Studies, we believe that the quality of education depends entirely on the expertise, passion, and dedication of those who teach. Our platform is supported by a diverse, international network of educators, teachers, trainers, mentors, leadership coaches, and subject specialists.

By bringing together global perspectives and real-world professional experience, our faculty ensures that learning goes far beyond textbooks—preparing students, teachers, and professionals for real-world success.

Who Makes Up Our Faculty?

Depending on the course and program, learners have the unique opportunity to learn and interact with:

International Teachers

Bringing global teaching methodologies and cross-cultural insights to every learning session.

Academic Professionals

University-level educators specializing in structured academic support, research, and scientific writing.

Subject Specialists

Experts in diverse fields ranging from Human Physiology and Pathology to Psychology and Digital Technology.

Professional Trainers

Industry practitioners sharing practical skills in digital marketing, AI tools, communication, and business startup.

Leadership Coaches

Experienced mentors guiding individuals toward personal growth, emotional intelligence, and transformational leadership.

Business Mentors & Guest Speakers

Industry leaders who provide practical guidance for entrepreneurs and career seekers.

Our Academic Leadership

Prof. Dr. Huma Shah

Professor | Pathology & Pathophysiology | Educator | Researcher | Mental Health Trainer | Leadership Coach | International Trainer

Prof. Dr. Huma Shah is an educator, academic professional, researcher, trainer, leadership coach, and international speaker with around 15 years of experience in education and teaching.

Her academic interests and professional work span areas including Pathophysiology, Physiology, Endocrinology, Neurology, Psychology, Mental Health, Education, Leadership, and Human Development.

Passionate about making education meaningful, practical, inclusive, and future-focused, Dr. Huma Shah aims to help learners develop academic excellence alongside confidence, communication, critical thinking, leadership, and practical life skills. Her professional journey includes associations with international initiatives such as The World Eye Club Global and Global Trainer Academy.

Her Educational Philosophy

“Education should not only prepare students for examinations; it should prepare them for life.”

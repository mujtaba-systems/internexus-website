About Founder

Prof. Dr. Huma Shah

Professor | Pathology & Pathophysiology | Educator | Researcher | Health Care / Mental Health Trainer | Leadership Coach | International Trainer

~15 Years of Academic & Teaching Experience
Associated with International Certified Platforms Worldwide
International Transformational Speaker

Prof. Dr. Huma Shah is an educator, academic professional, researcher, trainer, leadership coach, and international speaker with around 15 years of experience in education and teaching.

Her academic interests and professional work span areas including Pathophysiology, Physiology, Endocrinology, Neurology, Psychology, Health Care / Mental Health, Education, Leadership, and Human Development. She is passionate about making education more meaningful, practical, inclusive, and future-focused.

Through her work, Prof. Dr. Huma Shah aims to help learners develop not only academic knowledge but also confidence, communication, critical thinking, leadership, and practical life skills.

Core Expertise & Focus

Pathophysiology & Science Health Care / Mental Health Leadership Coaching Human Development
“Education should not only prepare students for examinations; it should prepare them for life.”

Prof. Dr. Huma Shah believes that every learner has potential, and the right education, mentorship, and opportunities can help transform that potential into meaningful achievement. Her vision for Internexus Global Studies is to create a dynamic and empowering learning environment.
